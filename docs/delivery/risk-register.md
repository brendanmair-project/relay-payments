## Status
Living document. Update as risks are resolved, downgraded or newly identified.

## How to use this
Each risk has a likelihood and impact rating (Low/Medium/High, qualitative) and a status. The owner (Brendan) reviews this file at the start of each roadmap phase and adds any newly discovered risk. Outcomes of owner decisions are recorded as ADRs.
